The interesting move is packaging institutional knowledge as skills rather than as a searchable corpus: Atomic Knowledge Units, structured and governance-aware, that make agents act correctly instead of merely answer correctly. Deployed at Yahoo with 67 engineers (2.6 hours saved per week, NPS +35). Skills are also a natural unit of privilege: you can grant a team a capability without granting them the underlying documents.
