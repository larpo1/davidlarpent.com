The architecture paper: specialised neural modules writing to and reading from a shared, limited-capacity workspace, with attention as the competition for access. The engineered version of exactly the structure Anthropic later observe emerging on its own inside a transformer. The bottleneck is the point — scarcity is what forces global coordination.
