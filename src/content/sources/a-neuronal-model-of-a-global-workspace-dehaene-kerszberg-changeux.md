The move from metaphor to mechanism. Dehaene and colleagues give the workspace a neuronal substrate: long-range cortical neurons that bind distributed processors into a coherent, sustained state. This is the "global neuronal workspace" — and its prediction that access involves dense long-range connectivity is exactly what Anthropic report as ≈100× connection density in the J-space.
