The canonical general statement: context is a finite resource with diminishing marginal returns, and the job is curating the smallest high-signal set of tokens that makes the desired behaviour likely. The organisational brain is this essay scaled up, the same curation discipline applied institutionally rather than per session, with the curator's judgement replaced by policy.
